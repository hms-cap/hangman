# BuyDopa

A pretend shopping app. Browse a catalog, catch flash deals and festival sales, add to cart, pay with play money and track orders that never arrive. Nothing is charged and nothing ships.

**Open:** https://hms-cap.github.io/hangman/buydopa/

Turn on **Tactic lens** (bottom right) to see the persuasion tactics labelled on the page. Cart, orders and wallet are saved in your own browser only.
