Paper Games

Five pencil-and-paper games. Your progress stays on this device.

Hangman

Guess the word one letter at a time before the drawing is finished.

Sudoku

4,000 puzzles, each with a single solution, from Easy to Expert. Includes notes, hints and undo.

Five Letters

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. A new daily word, plus unlimited practice.

Chess

Play the computer at five strengths, from Beginner to Expert, or pass the phone to a friend.

Word Trail

Swipe through a grid of letters to find every hidden word. A new grid every day, plus practice.

Install on your phone

Add Paper Games to your home screen and it opens full-screen like an app. After the first visit every game works offline.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then Install.
