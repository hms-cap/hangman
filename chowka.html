Changing these starts a new game.

Turn time limit

With a limit, a bar runs down on the player's card; when it runs out the game throws or makes a sensible move for them. Each player's clock always shows their total time.

How to play
  • Throw the four cowries. Count the shells lying mouth up: 1, 2 or 3 moves that many. All four up is chowka (4), none up is bara (8), and both give you another throw.
  • Tap the shells to throw straight away, or hold Shake to rattle them in your hands first: the moment you let go decides how they land. Every shell is still an even chance either way up.
  • When you stop throwing, spend each throw on one pawn: pick a number, then tap a pawn. Different throws can move different pawns.
  • Pawns start on your home square (the cross on your edge). They go round the outer ring anticlockwise, then the inner ring clockwise, then into the centre.
  • Land on the other player's pawn to capture it: it goes back to its home and you get another throw. Squares with a cross are safe: no captures there.
  • Pairs (jodi): two of your pawns on one square can move together on an even throw (2, 4 or 8), each going half of it. Pick the even number and tap Move pair, or just tap the pair when only the pair can use the throw. A moving pair can land on the other player's pair and captures both.
  • You can't go into the inner ring until you've captured at least once. Until then your pawns keep going round.
  • The centre needs an exact throw. A throw no pawn can use is lost. First to bring all four pawns to the centre wins.
