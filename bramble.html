How to play Bramble

A small word-tile game on a 9 × 9 garden bed. Build words with lettered tiles, score points, and finish with more than your opponent.

Your turn

  • You hold 7 tiles. Drag them onto the board, or tap a tile, then tap a square. Tap a placed tile (or drag it back) to take it off again. With a keyboard, pick a square, type letters (tap the square again to switch between across and down), Backspace to take one back and Enter to play.
  • Put all your tiles in one row or one column, with no gaps. The first word covers the centre star; after that, your tiles must join onto tiles already on the board.
  • Every word you make, along the line and across it, must be in the word list (at least two letters). A red outline and the reason tell you what's wrong when you press Play.
  • Before you play, the button shows what the move scores. After you play, you draw back up to 7.

Scoring

  • Each tile has a value (small corner number): common letters are 1, rare ones up to 10. A word scores the total of its tiles.
  • Leaf squares count for the tile on them that turn: 2L doubles it, 3L triples it.
  • Blossom squares count for the whole word: 2W doubles it, 3W triples it. If a word covers two, they multiply.
  • Squares only count for tiles you place that turn. Words you cross score too, with the same squares.
  • Play all 7 tiles in one turn for +30.
  • A blank tile is worth 0 but can be any letter you choose.

Stuck?

  • Exchange throws back any tiles for new ones (only while the bag still holds 7 or more) and ends your turn. Pass skips your turn. Both are in the buttons under the rack.
  • Show a play (against the computer, in the daily and in practice) reveals one of the best plays on the board. It counts in your stats. The bag count above the board opens the unseen tiles: every tile you haven't seen yet, which is handy for planning.

The end

  • When the bag is empty and someone plays their last tile, the game ends: that player gains the value of everyone else's leftover tiles, and the others lose it.
  • If six turns in a row are passes or exchanges, the game also ends and everyone loses the value of their leftover tiles.

Modes

  • vs Computer: four levels, Easy to Expert. Pass & play: two players on one device; a cover screen hides each rack between turns.
  • Daily: the same tiles for everyone, just you and 10 turns, no exchanging and no penalty at the end. Try to beat the day's target, which is what the Expert computer scores from the same tiles. Words flagged rare are valid but uncommon.
  • Practice: the same as the Daily, but as many games as you like, each with a new set of tiles and its own target. Past dailies replays an earlier day's tiles. Practice never changes your daily streak.

Which letter?

Exchange tiles

Tap the tiles to throw back. You draw the same number and your turn ends.
