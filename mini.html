How to play

A mini crossword: fill the 5 × 5 grid so every row and column spells the answer to its clue.

  • Tap a square to choose it. Tap it again, or tap the clue, to switch between Across and Down.
  • Type with the keyboard below; you move on through the word, then to the next clue. The arrows beside the clue step through the clues.
  • Stuck? Check crosses out wrong letters in a square, a word or the whole grid. Reveal fills in the answer (marked with a red corner).
  • Fill the grid correctly to finish. A solve with no checks or reveals is a clean solve.

There's a new puzzle every midnight, the same for everyone, and you can go back to any earlier one from the menu (tap the title) or the stats.
