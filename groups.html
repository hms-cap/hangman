Four Groups

Make four groups of four.

How to play

Sixteen words hide four groups of four. Find what links each group.

  • Tap four words you think belong together, then Submit.
  • You can make four mistakes. If three of your four words are right, you'll see One away.
  • Stuck? Hint names the easiest group you haven't found; tap again to mark one of its words, then a second.
  • Watch for decoys: some words seem to fit more than one group, but each puzzle has exactly one answer.

Levels. Easy uses plain categories with no decoys. Medium adds phrases like ___ ball and a decoy or two. Hard brings hidden words, anagrams and sounds, with more decoys.

Each group has a color, from the most straightforward to the trickiest:

EasiestMediumHarderTrickiest

There's a new daily puzzle at each level every midnight, the same for everyone. Practice has unlimited puzzles at each level, the hand-written classics and every past daily.
