How to play

  1. Swipe through neighboring letters to spell a word, in any direction, diagonals included. You can also tap letters one at a time, then tap the last letter again to enter the word.
  2. Words need 4 or more letters and can't reuse a tile.
  3. Find every target word to finish the grid. Targets are everyday words. Plurals, other word forms and rarer words still count as bonus words.
  4. The small number on a tile shows how many target words still to find start there. A tile is inked in once every target word that uses it has been found.
  5. Stuck? Hint shows the first three letters of a word and outlines them on the grid.
