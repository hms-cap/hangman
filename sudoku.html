How the rating works

Every puzzle has a difficulty rating, and you start with a rating that matches the level of your first rated game. Solve a puzzle and you score up to 1: mistakes, hints and guess-like moves (numbers no technique in the guide would have found) take points off, and solving quicker than usual for that puzzle adds a little. If you score more than expected for a puzzle that hard, your rating goes up; less, it goes down. Leaving a puzzle with five or more of your own numbers still on the board, or hitting the mistake limit, counts as a loss. Each puzzle is rated once, so replays don't count. The rating is marked with a ? until you have played 10 rated games, while it is still settling.
